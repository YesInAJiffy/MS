Think of every task and responsibility you have as a drop of water—and you’re the plant. These responsibilities are essential for your growth: caring for your family, cultivating your mental health, working, and now adding school to the mix. But if those drops come too fast and too heavy, and you don’t have a system in place to manage them, they can overwhelm you, saturating your roots and stalling your growth.

That's where the importance of scheduling comes in. It’s not just about staying organized—it’s about taking the time and creating the right conditions to thrive. That means intentionally setting aside time to meet with the people who are here to support you: your Student Success Coach, Course Instructor, Student Experience Specialist, or Tutor. By doing this, you control the flow of your responsibilities, allowing them to nourish your development instead of bogging you down.

In this section, you’ll schedule your first key session with your Student Success Coach. Get into the habit of setting reminders on your phone and adding meetings to your calendar. Stick with it. With structure and support, you'll be growing in no time.

At the end of the page, you’ll be asked to confirm that you’ve scheduled your first session with your Student Success Coach and that you understand how to schedule meetings with your Course Instructor and Student Experience Specialist when needed. Be sure to double-check that everything is set before moving on.

Schedule a conversation with your Student Success Coach

Select your Student Success Coach's name from the header menu on the Student Dashboard. Then schedule time with them in the next week or two. Be sure to start your courses while you prepare to meet with your Coach. All coaching sessions will take place over the video platform, Webex. To prepare for your first coaching session that will take place live and on camera, please download Webex here .

When you are in your course and have questions, you will be able to schedule time with your Course Instructor and/or Student Experience Specialist as needed.

Select courses offer additional support from a Course Instructor. Courses that don’t have a Course Instructor are supported by our Student Experience Specialists.

If you have a Course Instructor, select your Course Instructor’s name from the header menu on the Student Dashboard. You can schedule time with them during your course whenever you feel you need additional support.
If you are enrolled in a course without a Course Instructor, you will be supported by a Student Experience Specialist. They will reach out to you on an as-needed basis. Be sure to get started in your courses while you prepare to connect with your supporting staff.
Most courses also include 24/7 tutoring support through Tutor.com, so you can connect with a tutor any time you need help.
To sum it up: start strong by scheduling a meeting with your Success Coach now, and know that Course Instructor and Student Experience Specialist support will be there for you during your course if you need it.
